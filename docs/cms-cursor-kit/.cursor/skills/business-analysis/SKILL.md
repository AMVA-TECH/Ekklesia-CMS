# Business Analysis Skill

## Purpose
Discover and describe the real CrossLife business process before proposing software behaviour.

## Workflow
1. Identify the business problem, desired outcome and process owner.
2. Identify users/actors and their responsibilities.
3. Capture the AS-IS process without judging it prematurely.
4. Identify pain points, delays, duplication, control gaps and workarounds.
5. Describe a TO-BE process only after understanding constraints.
6. Capture triggers, inputs, steps, decisions, approvals, exceptions, outputs and notifications.
7. Identify data, source of truth, sensitivity, access and retention.
8. Define measurable outcomes and unresolved questions.
9. Recommend MVP, later phase or out of scope with rationale.

## Outputs
- Process register entry.
- AS-IS/TO-BE process map or structured workflow.
- Business rules.
- Stakeholder/process-owner list.
- Open questions and risks.
- Candidate requirements.

## Rules
Do not invent policies or authority. Do not assume a church process because another church uses it. Preserve CrossLife terminology. Distinguish what stakeholders said from the analyst's recommendation.

# Requirements Engineering Skill

## Purpose
Convert validated business needs into clear, testable and traceable requirements.

## Requirement format
- ID: `REQ-[AREA]-###`
- Name
- Type: functional / non-functional / business rule / constraint
- Statement: “The system shall…”
- Business rationale
- Actor and preconditions
- Main flow and alternative/error flows
- Data requirements
- Permission/privacy implications
- Acceptance criteria
- Priority and release recommendation
- Source/process owner
- Status: draft / validated / approved / deferred / rejected

## Quality checks
Requirements must be necessary, unambiguous, atomic, feasible, consistent, verifiable and traceable. Avoid vague words such as “easy”, “fast”, “secure” or “user-friendly” without measurable criteria.

## Traceability
Link each requirement to its business objective, process, design component, task and test. Do not mark approved without explicit authority.

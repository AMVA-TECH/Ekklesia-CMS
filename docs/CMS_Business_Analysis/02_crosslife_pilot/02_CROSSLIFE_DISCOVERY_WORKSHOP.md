# CrossLife Pilot — Stakeholder Discovery Workshop

## Objective

Validate the actual operating model before finalising the product configuration, workflows, access matrix or schema.

## Workshop 1 — Governance and organisational structure

### Questions
- Is there a board, council, elders' body, executive committee or other governing body?
- Who is the final decision-maker for doctrine, strategy, staffing, finance and operations?
- How are leadership positions created and approved?
- Who appoints, renews, suspends and removes leaders?
- Are appointments permanent, fixed-term, voluntary or paid?
- Which offices report directly to the senior pastor or governing body?
- Are there formal delegations when a leader is absent?
- Are decisions recorded in minutes or resolutions?

### Evidence to request
- Current organogram.
- Constitution/bylaws or governance policy, if available.
- Current leadership/office-holder list.
- Position descriptions.
- Appointment letters or records.
- Approval/delegation matrix.
- Relevant meeting/minutes templates.

## Workshop 2 — Departments, ministries, programmes and groups

For each unit, ask:
- What is its official name and category?
- What is its purpose and scope?
- Who leads it and who appoints the leader?
- Who can join and who approves membership?
- Does it have members, volunteers, staff or participants?
- Can it create events and communicate with its members?
- Does it have a budget or assets?
- Does it report activity or outcomes?
- Can a person belong to multiple units?
- Can one unit contain sub-units?

Do not treat every named activity as a department. Confirm whether it is a permanent unit, temporary project, recurring programme, event, team or committee.

## Workshop 3 — People and membership

- What is the difference between visitor, attendee, member, active member and inactive member?
- Is there a formal membership application or confirmation process?
- Are baptism, classes, orientation, covenant or other milestones relevant?
- Who verifies and approves membership?
- Are family/household relationships recorded?
- How are children and dependants represented?
- What personal data is mandatory?
- Who can view contact, family and pastoral-care details?
- How are transfers, resignations, deaths and status changes handled?
- How are duplicate records corrected?

## Workshop 4 — Events, attendance and projects

- What event/service types exist?
- Who can create and approve events?
- Is registration required or optional?
- How is attendance recorded: manual list, QR, paper, headcount or other?
- Are attendance records individual or aggregate?
- Who can correct attendance?
- How are volunteers assigned?
- What constitutes a project and how is participation tracked?
- What reports are required?

## Workshop 5 — Member portal and communication

- Which member details can a member edit directly?
- Which edits require administrator approval?
- Can members view a directory? What privacy controls apply?
- Can members request to join ministries/groups?
- Can members register for events?
- Which announcements are public to all members versus targeted?
- Which channels are currently used (SMS, email, WhatsApp, printed notices)?
- What consent and opt-out practices exist?

## Workshop outputs

- Validated organisation structure.
- Actor and responsibility register.
- Approved membership lifecycle.
- Unit classification and lifecycle rules.
- Event and attendance rules.
- Member visibility/privacy rules.
- Approval matrix.
- Prioritised requirements and acceptance criteria.
- Updated decision log and open-questions register.

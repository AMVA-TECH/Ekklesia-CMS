# Product Benchmark — Tanzania and International Reference Systems

## Purpose and caution

This benchmark identifies publicly advertised capabilities that can inform requirements. Marketing pages are not independent verification of security, reliability, actual customer adoption or feature quality. Validate current product claims directly before procurement decisions.

## Tanzanian references

### WatuChurch
Public website: https://watuchurch.com/

Publicly advertised areas include member profiles and family information, ministry assignments, baptism records, attendance, income/expenses, tithes, mobile-money/bank integrations, bulk SMS, events, budgets, prayer requests, visitor follow-up, cell groups, documents, projects and member self-service.

**Product lesson:** Broad operational coverage and targeted communication are expected by the local market. Avoid adopting every module without a validated pilot need.

### Kanisa MS (NetPoa)
Public website: https://netpoa.tz/kanisa

Publicly advertised areas include Swahili interface, TZS pricing, member/family records, QR/manual attendance, giving, expense request/approval/payment, department access, zones/deacons, pastoral visits, member portal, reports, SMS, leadership duties, self-registration and import tools.

**Product lesson:** Localisation, controlled self-registration, role separation, approval workflows and practical data migration matter.

### WauminiLink
Public website: https://wauminilink.co.tz/

Publicly advertised areas include members/families, leadership, finance, pledges/budgets/expenses, events, attendance, announcements, SMS, branches, roles, analytics, member portal, assets and bereavement records.

**Product lesson:** Member self-service and connected operational records are increasingly expected. Branch functionality is not required for the CrossLife pilot.

### Melian Church Management System
Public website: https://www.meliansoftware.com/church-management-system-tanzania

Publicly advertised areas include members, branches/cell groups, attendance, offerings/pledges, accounting, children’s ministry, SMS/WhatsApp and financial reports.

**Product lesson:** Group hierarchy and attendance are common capabilities; do not confuse a vendor's branch/cell terminology with the church's own structure.

### Kanisa Digital
Public website: https://kanisadigital.com/

Publicly advertised areas include member profiles, family connections, attendance, pastoral follow-up, giving, campaigns, services/events, SMS/WhatsApp, reports, mobile app and role-based access.

**Product lesson:** Member engagement, communication and pastoral follow-up complement core administration.

### SoftChurch
Public website: https://softecki.co.tz/church

Public marketing material mentions membership, giving, events, groups, mobile wallets, SMS/email/WhatsApp, member portal, accounting exports and mobile/API capabilities. Detailed claims should be verified with the vendor.

## International references

### Planning Center / Church Center
- https://www.planningcenter.com/
- https://churchcenter.com/

Useful reference areas: connected church operations, people, groups, services, registrations and member-facing self-service. Study how administrative workflows and member experience share consistent records.

### Tithely
- https://get.tithe.ly/

Useful reference areas: church engagement, communication, giving and connected administration. Treat it as a benchmark, not a feature checklist.

## Competitive baseline

A credible first release should cover:
- People and household records.
- Membership lifecycle.
- Leadership and organisational units.
- Ministry/group participation.
- Events and attendance.
- Member self-service.
- Announcements and basic communication.
- Roles, permissions, reports and audit.

Potential differentiators:
- Configurable church governance and organisational structures.
- Correct separation of office, department, ministry, life group, project and event.
- End-to-end, auditable workflows rather than disconnected CRUD screens.
- Strong member privacy and scoped access.
- Clean member portal tied to the operational records.
- Local usability, Swahili readiness and practical migration support.
- Integration-ready architecture for a later community application.

## Scope discipline

Do not add giving, full accounting, assets, payroll, advanced pastoral care, livestreaming or social networking to the first release solely because a competitor advertises them. Validate demand, operational readiness and legal/security implications.

# Functional Requirements Register

IDs are stable references. Priority and release assignment remain provisional until pilot validation.

## Organisation and structure
- FR-ORG-001: System shall support multiple independent church organisations.
- FR-ORG-002: Each organisation shall configure its profile and operational settings.
- FR-ORG-003: Authorised users shall configure organisational units using configurable categories.
- FR-ORG-004: System shall support optional unit relationships without requiring branch functionality in the CrossLife pilot.
- FR-ORG-005: System shall retain unit status and history.

## Governance
- FR-GOV-001: System shall support configurable leadership positions.
- FR-GOV-002: System shall record appointments with effective dates.
- FR-GOV-003: System shall support multiple appointments per person.
- FR-GOV-004: System shall record appointment decisions and history.
- FR-GOV-005: System shall keep application permissions separate from leadership titles.

## People and membership
- FR-PPL-001: System shall maintain person records independently of login accounts.
- FR-PPL-002: System shall support contact details and household relationships.
- FR-PPL-003: System shall support duplicate detection and controlled merging/correction.
- FR-MEM-001: System shall support configurable membership categories/statuses.
- FR-MEM-002: System shall support an application/review workflow when enabled.
- FR-MEM-003: System shall retain membership status history.
- FR-MEM-004: Member portal shall expose permitted membership information to the member.
- FR-MEM-005: Member shall be able to request correction of restricted profile information.

## Ministries and life groups
- FR-UNIT-001: System shall support ministries, departments, life groups and teams as distinguishable unit categories.
- FR-UNIT-002: System shall support unit leaders and co-leaders.
- FR-UNIT-003: Members shall discover units marked as visible.
- FR-UNIT-004: Members shall request to join where enabled.
- FR-UNIT-005: Authorised leaders shall review participation requests.
- FR-UNIT-006: System shall retain participation history.

## Events and attendance
- FR-EVT-001: Authorised users shall create and publish events/services.
- FR-EVT-002: Events shall support audience, venue, schedule and registration settings.
- FR-EVT-003: Members shall browse published events.
- FR-EVT-004: Members shall register where enabled.
- FR-ATT-001: Authorised users shall record attendance.
- FR-ATT-002: System shall distinguish individual attendance from aggregate headcount.
- FR-ATT-003: Members shall view their own attendance history.
- FR-ATT-004: Attendance corrections shall be permission-controlled and auditable.

## Projects and communication
- FR-PRJ-001: Authorised users shall create and manage projects.
- FR-PRJ-002: Members shall express interest in open volunteer opportunities.
- FR-PRJ-003: Project coordinators shall approve and assign participants.
- FR-COM-001: Authorised communicators shall publish announcements.
- FR-COM-002: Announcements shall support controlled audience targeting.
- FR-COM-003: System shall record notification/delivery outcomes where supported.

## Security and oversight
- FR-SEC-001: System shall support role-based permissions.
- FR-SEC-002: Permissions shall be scoped to organisation and, where appropriate, unit.
- FR-SEC-003: System shall record sensitive administrative actions in an audit trail.
- FR-REP-001: Authorised users shall access operational reports within their scope.
